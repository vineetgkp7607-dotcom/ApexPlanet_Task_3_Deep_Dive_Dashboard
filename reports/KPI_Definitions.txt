 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

| No. | KPI                        | Formula                      |
| --- | -------------------------- | ---------------------------- |
| 1   | Total Revenue              | SUM(Total_Sales)             |
| 2   | Total Orders               | COUNT(Order_ID)              |
| 3   | Average Order Value (AOV)  | Total Revenue / Total Orders |
| 4   | Total Customers            | COUNT(DISTINCT Customer_ID)  |
| 5   | Average Quantity per Order | SUM(Quantity) / Total Orders |
